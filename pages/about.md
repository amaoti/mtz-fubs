**About the Collection**  

This collection was assembled by Amaobi Otiji, a student of the Rutgers University Master of Information Program from 2023-2025, during the months of January to April 2025. This project was undertaken as a part of the the University of Idaho Library's [CollectionBuilder LIS Student Program](https://collectionbuilder.github.io/community/student-incentives.html). This initiative was conducted by the University of Idaho CollectionBuilder Team to foster engagement between the CollectionBuilder Framework and current LIS students. With initial support from the Institute of Museum and Libraries Services, whose work and support have had a significant positive impact on our country, eight students were selected to build collections on a topic of their choice to help improve and expand CollectionBuilder as well as engage with the topic of their choice.

**Statement on inclusion of content:**

The web archive preserves a snapshot of the source sites for future research and educational use under fair use and archival exemptions (17 U.S.C. §§ 107, 108). It supports documentation of the Mount Zion and Female Union Band Society cemeteries and their role in Black Georgetown history. The content present in the collection is not used commercially or monetized in any way. No ownership over the included content is claimed.
